# TGramSave privacy policy

_Draft. Fill in the contact email and publish at a public URL before store submission._

TGramSave is an unofficial browser extension for downloading media from Telegram Web. It is not affiliated with Telegram.

## What stays on your device
- Your settings (theme, folder, filters, limits).
- The list of media found on the Telegram Web page you are viewing (URL, name, type, size), so the popup can show it. Clear it any time with "Clear All" or by uninstalling.
- Your free-tier daily download counter.

Media is fetched directly from Telegram's servers using your own Telegram Web session and saved to your computer. TGramSave never sends Telegram messages, media or links to anyone.

## Premium (only if the Premium option is enabled in your version)
To check whether you have Premium, the extension talks to our server:
- A **random install id and secret** created on first run. They contain no personal information and no account is created.
- A **status check** (is this install premium, and until when).
- Your **email address, only if you type it into "Restore"**. We use it solely to match your Ko-fi payment to your install.

Payments are processed by Ko-fi under Ko-fi's own terms. We receive from Ko-fi: the payment amount, the plan or tier, your email, and the message you wrote (which holds your activation code). We keep this to provide and support Premium and do not sell or share it.

Retention: payment records and install ids are kept while Premium is offered; email us to have yours deleted.
